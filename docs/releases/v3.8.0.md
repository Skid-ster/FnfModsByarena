## Testing release

Five rebuilt songs for **Codename Engine**. Download **TrollgeMod.zip** and put
it in the engine's `mods/` folder, or extract it as `mods/TrollgeMod/`. The
versioned ZIP contains the same bytes. Psych Engine and YoshiCrafter are not
supported.

### What's changed

- Written melody pitches now survive harmonisation; chords follow the actual
  hard-chart onsets and correct bars. Suspensions and pad extensions are fixed,
  and the opening hook has been retimed.
- Section roles are aligned with chart bars, unison two-lane hands are voiced
  once, the continuous drone is keyed to each song, and the glitch bass holds
  pitch. Both song stems use a linked gain envelope to give their decoded
  equal-gain sum headroom.
- Freeplay compatibility lists, CRT camera sampling and battle visuals have
  been updated. The character behaviour extension is wired into the five
  Trollge phases.
- Research and technical limitations are documented in
  [music research](https://github.com/Skid-ster/FnfModsByarena/blob/v3.8.0/docs/music-research.md).

### Please test

This is a **testing release**: the developer could not audition it or run it in
Codename Engine here. Automated validation and spectral measurements are not
proof that songs sound scary or catchy, that Freeplay works on your engine build,
or that your GPU compiles the shaders. Try Story and Freeplay, all five songs,
the voices against the instrumental, cutscenes, visual effects and the ending;
report which song/time and your Codename version if something goes wrong. If
shaders are unsupported, the scripts are intended to fail soft, but that also
needs testing.
