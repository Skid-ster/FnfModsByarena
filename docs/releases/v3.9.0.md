## Super Scary Trollge v3.9.0

Download **TrollgeMod.zip** and put it in Codename Engine's `mods/` folder, or
extract it as `mods/TrollgeMod/`. The versioned ZIP contains the same bytes.
This mod is for Codename Engine, not Psych Engine or YoshiCrafter.

### Changes

- Corrected CRT camera-space coordinates and preserved transparent,
  premultiplied HUD pixels; the VHS pass preserves alpha as well. Camera filters
  installed by other scripts are preserved, and this stage removes its own
  filters on teardown.
- Adjusted both camera targets so wider singing frames stay within the
  projected play area; the caption position now follows virtual screen height.
- Includes the five rebuilt songs, Freeplay compatibility lists and battle
  presentation improvements from v3.8.0.
- Added GLSL compilation against a minimal Flixel-compatible preamble and
  modelled bounds checks for all 800 projected battle animation frames.

### Testing limitations

Automated validation, standalone shader compilation and framing models passed,
but this environment cannot run Codename Engine, test a GPU driver or audition
the music. These checks **do not prove** shader display, gameplay, Freeplay
ordering, positioning during camera motion, or how the music sounds. Please test
Story and Freeplay, all five songs, the effects and the ending on your engine
version. If there is a problem, report the song, timestamp and engine version.
